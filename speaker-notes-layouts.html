Your Topic Accent Line

Slide 4

Bullets on the left, visual on the right

  • The workhorse layout: bullets on the left, a visual on the right.
  • The .two.wide-r variant gives the visual more room; plain .two splits evenly.
  • The visual here is the CSS-only Venn — swap in any widget, chart image or diagram.
Slide 5

Three numbers that tell the story

  • Stat row — three big animated counters.
  • data-count is the target number; data-prefix / data-suffix wrap it ($, %, M, T…).
  • Counters animate once per slide visit; ?static in the URL snaps them to final.
Slide 6

Six ideas, one card each

  • Card grid — .cards.c3 is a 3-across grid (6 cards = 2 rows).
  • Each card: inline SVG icon + h3 + one-sentence p.
  • Icons are 24×24 stroke SVGs — swap the paths for anything.
Slide 7

A five-step process, left to right

  • Close with a .flow-cap one-liner under the flow.
