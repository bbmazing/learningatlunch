Learning at Lunch — Slide Layouts
Speaker notes · 9 slides
================================================

Slide 1 — Your Topic Accent Line
  • Cover slide. Replace the h1, meta block and program line with your topic.
  • The two-line h1 uses a <span class="red"> for the accent line.
  • Every element with data-r animates in when the slide becomes active; data-d staggers the delay.

Slide 2 — 
  • Quote slide — a dark slide with a big pull-quote. Use it to set the thesis of the session.
  • Swap the blockquote and cite. Keep it to one sentence.

Slide 3 — What we will cover today
  • Agenda slide — one .jcard per section of the talk.
  • The optional <span class="demo"> chip flags where a live demo lands.
  • Keep it to 3–5 cards so the row stays readable.

Slide 4 — Bullets on the left, visual on the right
  • The workhorse layout: bullets on the left, a visual on the right.
  • The .two.wide-r variant gives the visual more room; plain .two splits evenly.
  • The visual here is the CSS-only Venn — swap in any widget, chart image or diagram.

Slide 5 — Three numbers that tell the story
  • Stat row — three big animated counters.
  • data-count is the target number; data-prefix / data-suffix wrap it ($, %, M, T…).
  • Counters animate once per slide visit; ?static in the URL snaps them to final.

Slide 6 — Six ideas, one card each
  • Card grid — .cards.c3 is a 3-across grid (6 cards = 2 rows).
  • Each card: inline SVG icon + h3 + one-sentence p.
  • Icons are 24×24 stroke SVGs — swap the paths for anything.

Slide 7 — A five-step process, left to right
  • Process flow — numbered steps with arrows, for any pipeline or loop.
  • .flow.compact fits five steps; drop .compact for four or fewer.
  • Close with a .flow-cap one-liner under the flow.

Slide 8 — Section 1 — Section Title
  • Section divider — a dark interstitial that opens each major section.
  • The .prog-rail shows where we are; mark the current section with class="on".
  • Keep the lead line to one sentence.

Slide 9 — The one-line takeaway
  • Closing slide — recap on the left, what comes next on the right.
  • End with a thank-you and open the floor for questions.
