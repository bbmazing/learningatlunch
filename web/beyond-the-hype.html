Our setup

Here at OCBC Indonesia: on-premise

  • Public AI runs in someone else’s cloud. Bank data cannot go there.

Part 1 · The one idea

Autocomplete, on an enormous scale

  • One job: predict the next word.
  • Your phone keyboard does a tiny version of this.
  • It picks a word, adds it, and guesses again, one word at a time.
  • Every essay, email and answer is built this way.
The customer would like to open a savings ?

Pick “account”, add it, guess the next word. Repeat.

Part 1 · Strengths & limits

Brilliant and unreliable at the same time

Great at

  • Drafting and rewriting: emails, memos, reports
  • Summarising long documents into one page
  • Translation: Bahasa ↔ English, both ways
  • Explaining any concept at any level

Trips on

  • News after its training cutoff
  • Precise arithmetic. Check every number
  • Your customers and systems. It has never seen them
  • Inventing details with full confidence
Hallucination: it can cite an OJK rule that does not exist, with full confidence. Check its facts every time.

Part 1 · The catch

A plain chatbot hits three walls

Frozen in time

Its knowledge stops at the training cutoff. Ask about last week and it may guess.

Blind to your data

It cannot see your inbox, files or systems. Your world is invisible to it.

All talk

It writes text. It cannot search, send or do anything on its own.

Part 2 · Tools

Tool calling: give the model hands

  • The model does not need to know everything. It needs to pick the right tool.
  • A tool can be search, a calculator, a database, OCR.
  • The model thinks and writes. Tools fetch facts and act.
  • Walls one and three: down.
YouBerapa kurs USD hari ini?
⚙ calling tool: rate_lookup("USD/IDR") → 16,240
AIKurs USD/IDR hari ini ada di sekitar Rp 16.240. Ada jumlah yang mau Anda konversi?

11
Part 2 · Agents

An agent is a loop, not a chat

  • Give it a goal, not a question.
  • It plans the next step, acts with a tool, checks the result.
  • Then it decides: loop again, or done.
  • Example goal: “screen this company for negative news”. Many searches, then a report.
  • You will watch an agent run this exact loop in Demo 2.

Part 2 · Grounding

Ground it in your documents

Your documentscontracts, memos, minutes and policies, uploaded once
→
Find the pagesthe system pulls only the parts relevant to your question
→
Answer + citationswritten from those pages, with a pointer to each source
Q: What did we agree with the vendor on penalties?  A: Late delivery incurs 0.5% per day, capped at 10%1, waived for force majeure2.  Sources: 1 Contract v2, p. 14 · 2 Addendum, p. 3

Answer from the pages, cite the pages, say so when the answer is missing. Engineers call it RAG.

Part 4 · Using it well

Start this week, inside the rules

Start here

  • Summarise a long report into one page
  • Draft and polish emails, then edit in your voice
  • Translate documents, Bahasa ↔ English
  • Ask it to explain a term you did not catch

Rules of the road

  • No customer or secret data in public AI tools. Use the bank’s own
  • Check every fact and number before you send
  • AI output is a draft. What you send carries your name

The intern rule: fast help, but you review before it leaves your desk.
